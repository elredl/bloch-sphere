## How it plays

- Self-paced. Each box shows with a Next button; after Next the box goes.
- Each turn is its own step: Conditions 1 and 2 watch it play, Condition 3 makes it
  (grab the tip, turn it along the guide). Next shows once the turn is done.
- The measurement demo is a step too; re-preparations happen as the next box appears.
- Lesson times below order everything; Next jumps over any time left, so nobody waits.

If this is recorded, say it as written: |0⟩ is "ket zero", |1⟩ "ket one", |+⟩ "ket plus",
|−⟩ "ket minus", |+i⟩ "ket plus i", |−i⟩ "ket minus i", Hadamard (HA-da-mar).

## Points to review

- Revised script (Nov 2, 2026): one storyline. Height sets the odds; the direction around the equator (phase) is hidden until a gate like H turns it into height. Part 1 raises the question, Part 2 names phase, Part 3 shows interference, Part 4 makes the height rule quantitative.
- Boxes never say who makes a turn ("Next, X is applied"), so the text is the same where the learner makes the turn (Condition 3) and where it plays by itself.
- Turns that only returned the arrow to a starting point are silent re-preparations now, not steps. Condition 3 makes 21 turns, down from 28: a smaller dose of the agency manipulation.
- Pause 1 is answerable from the text ("exactly halfway down, zero and one are equally likely"); pause 2 from "like Z, it changed only the phase"; pause 3 sets up H, Z, H without its result; pause 4's formula and "height, not angle" give the rule, not the 60 degree answer.
- The boxes are longer on average. Read them in the headset and cut any box that takes over about 20 seconds.
- The θ/φ angle arcs are cut (itinerary open decision 1).
