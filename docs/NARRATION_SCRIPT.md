## Recording

- One file, exactly 900.0 s (15:00.0), mono WAV, 48 kHz.
- Each line starts at its time and must be finished by its end time. Lines with an
  on-screen cue (an arc, a re-preparation, the measurement demo) should land on it;
  "Watch" lines come just before the arc starts.
- The four pauses are digital silence, exactly as listed. No music or room tone in them.
- Calm, even pace: 91 words a minute while speaking (no line above 116).
- In the app: the clip goes in the Narration field of `LessonController` (Lesson scene);
  set it in `StyleLabSetup.BuildLessonScene` too, or the next scene rebuild clears it.
  The app refuses to start if the clip length differs from the timeline by more than 0.5 s.
  If a cue has to move, edit only the event times in `lesson_timeline.json`; segment and
  pause times are fixed (spec).

|+i⟩ "ket plus i", |−i⟩ "ket minus i", θ "theta" (THAY-ta), φ "phi" (FYE), Hadamard (HA-da-mar).

## Points to review

- No pause is recapped afterwards: the narration never says what a pause's operation did, so Conditions 1 and 2 get no correct answer that Condition 3 doesn't also see. The next segment just carries on from the visible state.
- Pause 2: segment 2 teaches the turn direction (right-hand rule; about z, counterclockwise seen from above). Without it the answer is a guess; with it the pause tests applying the rule.
- Pause 3: segment 3 shows H taking ket zero to ket plus and says it makes a superposition, but never says H undoes itself. That is what pause 3 tests.
- Pause 4: segment 4 says the chance follows the height of the tip, not the angle. That steers away from the 67 % answer without giving 75 %. Remove the last clause if that is too much help.
- Each pre-pause line names the coming operation ("Next, it will get a quarter turn toward ket one") but asks no question, so no condition gets a prediction prompt from the narration.
- Before pause 1 the narration says it stops for a minute after each part. This is the only description of the pauses, and it is true in every condition.
